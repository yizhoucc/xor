Two-Argument Activations as Differentiable Probes of Learned Feature Interactions

A bilingual evidence synthesis spanning reproduction, architecture transfer, causal operator analysis, optimization limits, and deployment cost.

32 registered comparisons 76/76 document claims verified 5-seed core evaluations Updated September 2026
English manuscript

Abstract

Two-argument activation functions replace a fixed scalar nonlinearity with a small shared network that maps two learned projections to one output. We study these modules as differentiable probes of useful feature interactions across image classification, reconstruction, language modeling, recurrent memory, residual networks, and reinforcement learning.

A complete 40-condition causal matrix shows that the learned operator depends on the surrounding network and optimization basin. Every probe placed in a Bilinear-GLU host recovers multiplication, while every probe placed in a SwiGLU host recovers a scaled SwiGLU surface, independent of the probe initialization. The module therefore reveals an operator compatible with the trained host rather than converging to one universal activation.

Across tasks, InnerNet improves models that lack a built-in interaction or bypass path, but provides little benefit where residual structure already supplies one. Warm-start experiments separate optimization from expressivity: InnerNet matches the capacity of SwiGLU, while from-scratch training often fails to reach the same basin. A hidden-width ablation shows that performance saturates near width 16-32, whereas runtime rises sharply. These results support learnable activations as architecture probes and hypothesis generators, with deployment requiring subsequent distillation into efficient fixed operators.

Central claim

InnerNet can expose and refine useful bivariate operators inside a trained architecture. The recovered operator is conditional on the host network and optimization path.

Supported by 40/40 causal conditions, multi-seed functional results, and closed-form surface fits.

Evidence snapshot: 40/40 causal conditions recover the host operator family; InnerNet wins or ties 10/11 warm-start tasks; the constrained recurrent model reaches 98.44%; the current unfused Transformer implementation is 12.02× slower than SwiGLU at inference.

1. Motivation and contributions

The original two-argument activation study evaluated MLP and CNN classifiers on MNIST and CIFAR-10, reporting faster learning, modest accuracy gains, and robustness improvements. Our study asks a broader question: what architectural interaction does a trainable bivariate activation recover when placed inside different networks?

Contributions

  1. A multi-architecture evaluation that maps where learned two-input interactions help and where they do not.
  2. A causal matrix showing that the recovered operator family is controlled by the host basin rather than the InnerNet initialization.
  3. Warm-start and frozen-network tests that separate representational capacity from optimization difficulty.
  4. A quantitative account of parameter efficiency, runtime cost, and closed-form distillation.

2. Method

For each activation site, two learned projections produce scalars a and b. A small shared MLP computes f(a,b). The shared function can be visualized over a two-dimensional grid and fitted to candidate operators such as multiplication, SwiGLU, or low-order polynomials.

1 · InsertReplace a fixed activation or gated interaction with a shared two-input network.
2 · OptimizeTrain end-to-end, warm-start from a known host, or freeze the outer network.
3 · Read outSample the learned two-dimensional surface and fit simple operator families.
4 · ValidateCompare seeds, hosts, positions, scales, and deployment costs.

3.4 Capacity and optimization

Warm-start results separate what InnerNet can represent from what it can discover from scratch. Across 11 warm-start tasks, InnerNet wins or ties SwiGLU in 10. The controlled frozen-network experiment gives 77.38±0.51 PPL for InnerNet and 77.38±0.54 for SwiGLU. The from-scratch deficit is therefore an optimization barrier in this setting.

4. What operator is learned?

Single checkpoints often resemble a scaled SwiGLU. The d=64 Transformer surface is explained by 0.321 · SiLU(a) · b with R²=0.977. The causal matrix shows why this observation must be interpreted conditionally.

HostProbe conditionsRecovered familyFitConclusion
Bilinear GLUjoint/frozen × random/multiply × 5 seeds20/20 multiplicationR² 0.9976-0.9993Host preserves bilinear basin
SwiGLU4 initializations × 5 seeds20/20 scaled SwiGLUR² 0.9853-0.9912Initialization does not determine the endpoint
The learned surface is a readout of a host-compatible interaction basin. It is not evidence for one network-independent optimal activation.

Compute and distillation

On the same RTX 2080 Ti, InnerNet has only 1.17× the profiled major-operation FLOPs of SwiGLU, yet its unfused implementation is 17.33×/12.02× slower at CNN/Transformer inference and 7.63×/6.27× slower for a training step. Peak training memory is 7.90×/6.43× higher. The bottleneck is execution structure and intermediate materialization.

In the CNN deployment study, a fixed polynomial approximation runs 2.68× faster than InnerNet and remains above ReLU/SwiGLU accuracy, but loses 3.63 points relative to the learned module. This is a partial deployment closure rather than a lossless conversion.

7. Conclusion

Learnable two-argument activations are most useful as controlled probes of feature interaction. They can recover compact operators, reveal where a network benefits from multiplicative structure, and separate optimization barriers from capacity limits. The evidence does not support universal activation superiority or direct large-scale deployment. A practical workflow is to train the flexible probe, identify a stable operator family, and replace it with an efficient fixed approximation when the accuracy-cost tradeoff is acceptable.

中文摘要

这项工作的主线已经清楚：InnerNet 是一个可微的架构探针。它让网络自己学习两个特征之间的局部算子。训练结束后，我们读取二维函数表面，再把它拟合成乘法、SwiGLU 或低阶多项式。

最关键的因果结果来自 40 个条件。Bilinear host 中的 20 个条件全部回到乘法；SwiGLU host 中的 20 个条件全部回到 SwiGLU-like 表面。不同初始化不会改变这个归属。外围网络和优化 basin 才是决定因素。

因此，论文不应把 InnerNet 写成一个更强、更快的 activation。现有实现很慢，大模型从头训练也经常输给 SwiGLU。更稳妥的价值是：先用灵活的 InnerNet 探索局部交互，再把稳定结构提炼成简单算子。

一、论文主线怎么串起来

  1. 原论文起点：MLP/CNN 上略优于 ReLU，收敛更快。
  2. 我们扩展范围：Transformer、LSTM、RNN、AE、ResNet、RL 等。
  3. 我们找到边界：位置、规模、初始化和优化路径决定结果。
  4. 我们形成新定位：用可学习激活探测架构基元，再提炼部署。
一句话版本：InnerNet 能读出当前网络 basin 里有用的二元交互，但不会自动找到一个对所有网络都最优的 activation。

2.5 Sequential MNIST 的功能性结果

Plain RNN 和单 InnerNet 都只有约 11%。加入加法记忆通道和两个 InnerNet 后，约束版用 21K 参数达到 98.44±0.22%，8/9 个实际训练 seed 成功。

这个实验支持“加法记忆 + 二元交互在功能上足够”。单个 InnerNet 的表面无法稳定对应标准 input/output gate，因此不写成门结构再发现。
